## Does not reproduce

"Run-to-run variance below 0.001" (`triage-outcome-4_stage-27.json`). That holds only for the ten-family average. With byte-identical prompts, single family-level values move up to **0.0535** between runs (0.177 on exploratory items). This is twice the width of the smallest load-bearing interval, though it cannot manufacture the verdict.

## Questions the artifacts force

1. Two of the three "replications" of the anchor are **bit-identical** (48/50 cells), so the "third time" is a deterministic recomputation, not independent evidence.
2. The re-run stance pairs are matched by **word count** (7v7, 6v6) but differ by 2 tokens, and only one pair shares a verb class — the quoted design text claims both for both.
3. The register-matched arm is labelled "revivable" though its difference interval spans zero (+0.109, [−0.171, +0.408]).
4. Why do exactly 2 of 50 cells drift between two runs while 48 are bit-identical?
