# STEER - one decision point, dynamically named. Never issued generic.

Two forms, same named content. Which form works better is an open test
(P-f1 in PREDICTIONS.md): default to Q; D is the comparison arm.

Q: "Does {inherited term or feature} apply here - what must exist for
   {term} to make sense, and does it exist in {this domain or case}?"

D: "Check that {inherited term or feature} applies here: name what must
   exist for {term} to make sense, and verify it exists in {this domain
   or case}."

{term} is the specific inherited thing - a concept, a header structure, a
vocabulary, a design element. The naming is the load-bearing half.
