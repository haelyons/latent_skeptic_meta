The original study's ripple (Gurnee et al., "When Models Manipulate Manifolds", Transformer Circuits, Oct 2025; Claude 3.5 Haiku) lived at ambient dimension about 6. The working reading: at 410m the count code is too low-dimensional for Gibbs-style ripple to exist. That makes the next decidable question the PRECONDITION, one model-size rung up.

Question: at the final pretraining checkpoint of EleutherAI/pythia-1.4b-deduped (HuggingFace revision step143000, base model, no interventions - one cell; sweeps, other models, causal tests out of scope), measured with the same committed instrument: (1) does the count curve exist at all (a floor gate, defined below - no external anchor exists for this model, unlike 410m); (2) does the count representation's ambient dimension at the anchor layer reach 4 or more at any probe window (GUESS threshold, motivated by Haiku's 6 against 410m's 1-3 - the regime where the capacity-truncation account predicts a resolvable ripple); and (3) does any window clear the maximum-power shuffle null, with the invariance fork over clearing windows? Corrections, from the seed D2.1 post record (records/seedD2_1_post_elicit_qtriage.json):

1. Floor gate replacing the anchor gate: no reproduction ever measured this model, so the gate is existence, not replication - held-out R-squared pooled over widths 20-140 must reach at least 0.60 at some layer (GUESS floor: the 410m instrument gave 0.78 twice; the reproduction's smaller Pythia-160m gave 0.744 and GPT-2 gave 0.363, so 0.60 separates curve-exists from curve-absent). Below it, every ripple result is void and the rung is recorded as CURVE_TOO_WEAK. This gate is weaker than 410m's replication anchor and says so.

2. The token-position control's decision gains a no-signal branch: when NEITHER labelling clears its null, the decision is NO_PATTERN_TO_ATTRIBUTE - never a "distinguished" verdict over two absent patterns (seed D2.1's records read CHARACTER_COUNT_DISTINGUISHED at windows where nothing cleared).

3. The token label window's ceiling rule is committed here (it was a runner constant): a token count enters the window only with at least 40 training positions; counts above the ceiling are excluded and the ceiling is recorded next to the observed maximum, so the cut is visible (seed D2.1 cut 41 of 50, 56 of 69, 76 of 94 without a stated rule).

4. Recorded, not gated, at the anchor layer and window 140 only: within-bin Pearson correlation for bins of 35 labels across 1-140 (the same construct as the range check, on the well-represented window). Seed D2.1's width-280 bins showed fine resolution collapsing early even inside the fitted range (0.648 then 0.219, 0.153, 0.271); whether the same collapse holds at width 140 bears on whether a fine-scale ripple was ever measurable, and no run has recorded it.
