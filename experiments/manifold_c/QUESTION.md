Two prior results frame this question. First, the original study (Gurnee et al., "When Models Manipulate Manifolds", Transformer Circuits, Oct 2025; Claude 3.5 Haiku) found a curve in the residual stream tracking characters since the last newline, showing faint off-diagonal bands ("ringing") in its cosine-similarity structure, explained as Gibbs-style truncation: a similarity structure realizable in 150 dimensions forced into about 6. Second, a completed run in this project (seed D1, 2026-08-21; its committed numbers are restated here, and this file is their carrier) measured, on the final checkpoint of Pythia-410m-deduped: held-out probe R-squared 0.781 at layer 6 of 24 (embedding output is index 0); ambient dimension 1 at 95 percent variance (first component 95.3 percent); no ripple separable from a label-shuffle null at the single testable period (rank 109 of 200 at period 60); and a full-spectrum re-read showing the largest spectral power at period 120 - the profile's own length - which could not be ranked against any null because only in-band maxima were saved.

Seed D1's negative was censored by its own aperture: with ambient dimension 1, the theory's predicted ripple spacing equals the probe window divided by one - the window itself - at any window. Widening the window moves the prediction with it. So the decidable question is not "is there a ripple in a wider window" but invariance:

Question: at this same checkpoint, when the similarity structure of the character-count representation is measured at three apertures - probe windows of 140, 200, and 280 characters - and a periodicity clears its shuffle null at any aperture, is its wavelength INVARIANT in characters across apertures (an absolute property of the model's code), or PROPORTIONAL to the aperture (an artifact of the probe window)? And if no aperture clears its null, that is a decisive negative across every scale up to 280 characters - with one pre-registered escape, the represented-range check below, which decides whether the wide apertures were measuring anything at all.

Substrate, one cell only: EleutherAI/pythia-410m-deduped, HuggingFace revision step143000, base model, no interventions. The checkpoint sweep, the equilibrium-versus-fossil question, other models and sizes, and causal tests are out of scope. The downloaded config is authoritative; 24 layers and hidden size 1024 are MEMORY (Pythia paper, arXiv 2304.01373, table 1); mismatch is a halt. Layer indices count the embedding output as index 0; the best layer's location is recorded, never gated.

Definitions, fixed here:

1. Labels as in seed D1: a token's label is the count of characters on its line up to and including its last character, restarting after each newline; standalone newline tokens land at width plus one (measured fact, seed D1: 18 such positions). The label window per aperture is fixed by the calibration stage.

2. Curve readout: ridge regression from the residual stream after each layer to the label, fit on train documents pooled over all nine widths, R-squared on test documents, per layer. Ridge strength from calibration; grid must extend beyond 1000 (seed D1's grid ended at 1000 with quality still rising, delta 0.0033 - measured, results_seedD calibration record).

3. Represented-range check, at the best layer: held-out R-squared computed separately within each bin of 35 consecutive label values at aperture 280 (eight bins), where the bin's own variance is the denominator. Pre-registered reading, by rule not by value: a bin counts as represented when its within-bin held-out fit exceeds the 95th percentile of its own within-bin label-shuffle null (50 draws); the bin straddling 150 is recorded but excluded from the decision. If every bin fully at or below 140 is represented and every bin fully above 150 is not, counts beyond 150 are "not represented", and a null ripple at apertures 200 and 280 is then recorded as RANGE_NOT_REPRESENTED, not as evidence against the ripple. Context: the Pile's hard-wrapped training pressure sits near 70-72 characters per line (Gutenberg 2.17 percent of the mix; source: counting_manifold_formation_proposal.md, verified table).

4. Ripple statistic, per aperture A in {140, 200, 280}, at the best layer, test documents of width A only: per-label mean vectors; subtract the grand mean; cosine similarity matrix; off-diagonal profile m(delta) for delta 1 to max_delta (the calibration stage fixes max_delta per aperture and writes it into its committed record before any test-document number exists); subtract the best-fitting degree-2 polynomial (fixed here - seed D1 verified that a moving average removes exactly the predicted periods); discrete Fourier transform; the statistic is the MAXIMUM power over ALL whole-cycle periods from 4 characters to the profile length. No search band exists in this design - the band censorship of seed D1 is removed. Ambient dimension is measured and recorded per aperture; unlike seed D1 it constrains nothing.

5. Null, per aperture: labels shuffled within each document, 200 draws (GUESS), the same maximum-power statistic per draw. Separable means the observed maximum exceeds all but the top 5 percent of draws (policy, unchanged from seed D1). Every draw's FULL per-period power vector is saved, and the per-label mean vectors at the best layer are saved into the fetched results - both absences made seed D1's geometry unauditable afterward.

6. Invariance rule, over the apertures that clear their null: wavelengths in characters. All pairwise differences at most 10 characters (GUESS): INVARIANT. All wavelength-to-aperture ratios within 0.1 of each other (GUESS): PROPORTIONAL. Neither: MIXED. Exactly one aperture clears: UNDETERMINED. The headline decision is this fork, not mere presence; three 5-percent tests carry about a 14 percent family-wise false-positive rate, which the fork absorbs because it conditions on which apertures cleared.

7. Token-position control, per aperture: the same maximum-power statistic and null with tokens-since-newline labels; periods converted to characters at the measured characters-per-token; the character-count ripple is credited only where wavelengths differ by more than 3 characters (GUESS) or the two labelings disagree on clearing.

8. Anchor gate, by rule not by value. The anchor statistic is held-out fit at width 140 alone, judged at the layer the calibration stage selects on train documents at width 140 alone - the selection procedure is part of the anchor's definition, fixed here. The band is centred on the prior committed value 0.781; its half-width is measured, not guessed: 3 times the median absolute deviation of the anchor statistic across 5 resplits of the train documents (variance source: resamples of documents; rerun dispersion is zero for a deterministic probe, and a measured dispersion of exactly zero means the wrong source was chosen - halt WRONG_VARIANCE_SOURCE rather than emit a zero-width band). The band is computed and committed by the calibration record before any test-document row is read. A fresh-text anchor outside the measured band voids every ripple result as an instrument fault and blocks any next seed; it is not evidence about the model. The dispersion record ships with the results either way.

Dropped from seed D1, deliberately: the fitted-probe cross-check (its agreement was vacuous with one searchable period, its fit never converged, and no null was computable for it - all three measured in seed D1's post records).

Changed from the prior asking of this question, deliberately: the anchor band was a guessed plus-or-minus 0.08 and is now a measured-dispersion rule with its selection procedure pinned; the represented-range thresholds were guessed absolute values (0.5, 0.3) and are now per-bin shuffle nulls with the straddling bin excluded; each aperture's profile length must be committed by the calibration record before the run, not decided in setup notes. Everything else is unchanged on purpose, so the two askings differ only where the prior run's own failure pointed.
